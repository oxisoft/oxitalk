Links to Matrix rooms open in OxiTalk. Tap a matrix.to or matrix: link in another app or your browser, and OxiTalk goes straight to that room, or offers to join it if you are not in it yet. Links to a message open the room at that message, and links to a person show their profile. Links in your chats now open in OxiTalk too. On Android, turn on matrix.to links once in Settings, Messages.
