OxiTalk uses much less memory: chats full of photos no longer pile up memory while you scroll and switch between them, so your computer or phone stays fast after days of use. Photos you send now come with a small preview, so they show faster for the people you send them to. On desktop, GIFs move when you point at them. Your own picture and name changed in another app now show at once, and a chat with one other person shows their picture.
